# Figure notice

Primary-source figures belong to their paper authors, publishers or model developers. They are not covered by this repository's Apache 2.0 license. Attribution appears in [CREDITS.md](CREDITS.md) and beside each model.

Figures are included for architectural comparison and scholarly commentary. PDF excerpts remove unrelated page content; rasterization and resizing preserve technical content. Sample-image grids are not used as architecture figures. Inclusion does not imply endorsement.

Generated SVG diagrams are original editorial summaries. Source-based architecture diagrams reconstruct documented modules and connections from the cited primary source; input/output diagrams summarize catalog metadata when internal architecture is undisclosed. Task and variant qualifications still apply.

For corrections, attribution updates or removal requests, identify the model and affected asset as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../../CONTRIBUTING.md). Removal requests from rights holders are honored by replacing the figure with an input/output diagram.
